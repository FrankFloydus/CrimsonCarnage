# First-person controller

Apply the [project instructions](../AGENTS.md) and [Godot conventions](../docs/godot_conventions.md).
The implemented responsibilities and editable assets are recorded in [Architecture](../docs/architecture.md).

- Keep `first_person_controller.gd` as the input coordinator. It requests Sprint, Jump and Crouch through the same AbilitySystem as combat. The base player grants those abilities, Encumbered and Stamina Exhaustion. The caster also grants Fireball. `FirstPersonMovement` owns velocity, gravity, launch feasibility and `move_and_slide()`. Call its movement step once per physics tick.
- Resolve stance before movement. Enter crouch only with current floor or stair support and no jump request that tick. Discard airborne toggle presses, and let One Shot follow held input once grounded. Crouched players can jump or request standing in the air. Crouch owns the per-instance capsule and the saved `Look` pivot height. Keep the capsule bottom fixed and test the full authored standing volume with the body's collision mask and exceptions. Publish `State.Crouched` from actual stance. A held release can request standing while clearance is blocked, with the tag present until the capsule stands.
- Use the per-controller gait helper to resolve effective movement from the active Sprint ability, walk input and physical stance. Sprint is hold Shift. Held Ctrl and crouch temporarily override sprint gait. Release, exhaustion or encumbrance ends Sprint. Preserve the fresh press requirement after a blocked or interrupted sprint.
- Movement Speed is the actor's bonus percentage (normal 0%, minimum −100%, maximum +25%), read live through Movement.attributes. Apply the normal stat modifier pipeline, then multiply Settings.normal_run_speed (4 m/s default) by max(0, 1 + bonus/100) and one gait ratio. Normal Run Speed under Movement > Settings > Movement Baseline is the sole physical baseline owner; do not add a JSON duplicate. Add Flat 1 gives +1%; Add Flat 20 gives +20%; Increased Percent scales the accumulated bonus, so +20 flat and 20% increased give +24% while increased alone leaves zero unchanged. F3 displays bonus percent, never m/s or an implicit 100-point total. The reusable player scene owns one ActorAttributes node; the caster inherits it. Keep Normal Run Speed, Walk/Sprint/Crouch multipliers, jump heights, acceleration and terrain tuning in default_movement_settings.tres, referenced only by Movement. **Movement > Settings** is their single Inspector owner. Run uses multiplier 1, crouch-walk multiplies Walk and Crouch. Do not keep a second absolute run-speed baseline or mutate settings for stance/buffs. Pass the gait helper current_gait into Movement.move(delta, input, gait). Jump execution authorizes a takeoff, and jump height applies only at launch.
- The saved Sprint execution owns Stamina per Charge and Charge Interval (2 and 0.1 s). Check affordability of the next full charge before sprint travel. Movement reports actual grounded horizontal travel with input and retains unpaid time across activations. Sprint commits due charges through the shared resource API. A paid charge consumes any positive remainder below the next full charge as part of that same debit. The resulting zero triggers ordinary exhaustion. An underfunded start or voluntary release with Stamina left does not trigger the delay. Idle, walk, crouch, fully blocked motion and airborne travel add no chargeable time. Jump is blocked while Sprint is active. Sprinting off a ledge carries existing momentum into the fall, and air steering cannot accelerate above the normal air cap.
- The base player's Attributes node authors the carry comparison and its enter and exit events. The [Encumbered action](../docs/gas_framework_audit.md#encumbered-migration) listens and owns the effect instance. Keep the start and interruption rules on the Sprint definition. Movement reads load for the cubic speed factor and linear jump-height factor. Both reach full penalty at the authored ratio (default 2), with a default speed floor of 0.5 and zero jump height. Apply height at takeoff before calculating launch velocity. F3 reads the tag and Movement's factors.
- Preserve explicit physics ordering. Controller priority −10 precedes ActorAttributes regeneration at 0. ActorAttributes advances recovery once. The base controller authors the Stamina blockers. Inherited player scenes, including the Playground caster, inherit that list so active Sprint and the exhaustion effect pause recovery. Health and Mana recover while alive. A positive-to-zero Stamina transition publishes `Event.Stamina.Depleted`. The event-activated Stamina Exhaustion ability applies the retained effect, which grants `State.Stamina.Exhausted` for its authored duration. Require a fresh Shift press after insufficient funds or an interruption.
- Keep collision stance immediate and smooth the eye pivot in Crouch's render process using the crouch settings' transition time. Reverse from the displayed height, cap a descending eye beneath ceilings, and preserve independent pitch, roll, and stair compensation.
- Attempt stair clearance through the per-movement `FirstPersonStairStepper` before that single `move_and_slide()` call. The helper applies vertical lift only; preserve the requested horizontal velocity for normal collision movement and the one-tick support state after a successful step.
- Keep runtime state on component and helper instances. The controller owns its gait helper, while Movement owns its stair-stepper helper. The settings resources are shared authored data; do not mutate them to store velocity, coyote timers, or camera state.
- The body origin is at the character's feet. Standing capsule and eye height are authored in `first_person_controller.tscn`; crouched dimensions and input mode are authored in `default_crouch_settings.tres`.
- Body yaw, `Look` pitch, and `Look/Camera3D` roll are separate transforms. Preserve this separation when changing camera behavior.
- Keep stair presentation on the saved `Look/Camera3D`. Its compensation translates along world up, observes collision clearance, and is driven by the scene's `terrain_advanced` and `terrain_reset` connections. Preserve support-plane checks so continuous ramps, jumps, and unsupported landings do not receive stair compensation.
- Use the movement settings' custom gravity, independent of the project physics gravity. Do not introduce implicit built-in gravity into this controller.
- Keep the saved controller reusable: playground geometry and environment belong in `levels/playground/`, outside the player scene.

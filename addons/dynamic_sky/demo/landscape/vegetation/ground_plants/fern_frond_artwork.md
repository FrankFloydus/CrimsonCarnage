# Original fern frond artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool generated these two original 1024 × 1536 RGB masters. The files are copied unchanged into the project. No downloaded plant imagery is included. The open, twice-divided blade and serrated pinnules were informed by the [University of Washington Burke Herbarium](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Athyrium+filix-femina) and the [New York Botanical Garden's species description](https://sweetgum.nybg.org/science/world-flora/monographs-details/?irn=4981).

| Master | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [lady_fern_green.png](fronds/lady_fern_green.png) | Slender fresh frond with open, finely divided pinnae |
| [lady_fern_mature.png](fronds/lady_fern_mature.png) | Fuller mature frond with staggered pinnae and occasional muted brown tips |

The masters use a black matte. `fern_frond.gdshader` estimates coverage from the maximum linear RGB signal, clips the black background and uses alpha-to-coverage on MSAA edges. The original RGB remains the color source. There is no baked alpha channel, so bitmap alpha-border correction, premultiplication and alpha coverage preservation are disabled. Both masters import losslessly with mipmaps enabled and use anisotropic filtering. The authored frond meshes retain their original UVs in the shadow pass: automatic LOD and separate shadow-mesh generation are disabled in favor of their saved near and middle meshes.

The mesh generator assigns the two source fronds using UV ranges, mirrors some source coordinates, and bends each blade around its own curved rachis. The original masters contain no runtime shadows or lighting multiplier. The material receives native lights and GI and uses no emission.

## Generation prompts

**Slender frond**

> Create a high resolution original botanical material photograph of ONE complete, exceptionally delicate and lacy LADY FERN FROND (Athyrium type), isolated on perfectly solid pure black RGB(0,0,0), from a perpendicular overhead camera. Entire frond visible, central thin straight rachis vertical, its exposed short stalk at the bottom, acuminate tip near the top. The frond occupies roughly 85 percent of image height and 65 percent of width, with black padding all around. Its blade is twice divided: about twenty primary pinna pairs branch from the rachis, and EACH primary pinna consists of many individually separated slender serrated pinnules. Black background MUST remain clearly visible BETWEEN all primary pinnae and between the little pinnules. Feathery open lace, over forty percent black gaps within the overall blade envelope. No broad continuous solid lamina, no simple round oval leaves, no printed fern pattern on a solid leaf. A recognizable real freshly picked woodland fern, with irregular naturally staggered pinnae, unequal edges and occasional tiny curls, tapering toward both base and tip. Restrained matte medium olive green, subtle natural vein and age variation, slender pale green to straw rachis. Diffuse cross-polarized albedo photography, uniform neutral illumination without shiny highlights, directional shading or cast shadows. No perspective, no depth of field, no ground, no roots, no pot, no other plants, no text or labels. This is original source art for a realistic game fern with true fine leaf silhouettes.

**Mature frond**

> Original realistic game foliage source photograph: ONE complete mature woodland lady fern frond on a perfectly solid PURE BLACK background, photographed flat from perpendicular overhead. Do not make an illustration. Keep the thin rachis mostly straight and vertical, with a short exposed pale olive/straw petiole at the bottom and a gently curled acuminate tip. Entire frond fully visible with 4 percent padding at top/bottom and black side margins. The overall blade is long elliptic-lanceolate, broadest near the middle, markedly shorter lower pinnae and gracefully narrowing tip. Delicate real twice-pinnate anatomy: 18–23 irregularly staggered major pinna pairs, each formed from many slender deeply serrated pinnules, with clear black gaps between pinnae and small leaflets. This is an open feathery structure, not a solid leaf covered in a fern pattern. Natural asymmetric broken rhythms, some small missing or curled leaflets, slightly unequal pinnae. Matte diffuse albedo under uniform neutral cross-polarized lighting: restrained slightly older woodland olive green with fine vein variation, very occasional quiet brown tips on the oldest lower pinnae, no fluorescent green, no yellow wash, no heavy dead foliage. No cast shadows, no highlights, no haze, no ground, no pot, no other plants, no labels, no text. Keep black background truly black even in all the interior gaps. Source art must carry the fine real botanical silhouette and restrained natural material variation; it will be bent into an original three-dimensional fern mesh.

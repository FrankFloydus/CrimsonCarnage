# Close ground plants

These original plants add a local understory to the demonstration landscape. The individual `.tscn` files provide editable material, wind, weather, and visibility settings. `build_ground_plants.py` saves the modeled shrub, herb, grass and earlier fern OBJ meshes. `build_fern_fronds.py` saves the curved frond meshes used for the demo's ferns.

`lady_fern_frond` and `young_fern_frond` have lower arching outer fronds, a more upright center and modeled basal stems. Their cutout material uses two original ImageGen frond sources with finely divided pinnules and varied olive colors. [Artwork prompts and import settings](fern_frond_artwork.md) The previous `lady_fern` and `young_fern` scenes retain their individually modeled pinnules and remain available. `bilberry_shrub` supplies small leaves on upright stems, `wood_sorrel` supplies a low trifoliate herb, and `fescue_bunch` and `sedge_tussock` provide two different folded grass silhouettes. `meadow_panicle` has branched stalks with modeled florets. The names describe botanical forms rather than surveyed species reconstructions.

The modeled plant ShaderMaterials own leaf, dry-leaf and stem colors, roughness, transmission, and vein detail. The frond ShaderMaterial owns its two original color inputs and transmitted fraction. Both shaders support existing wind and accumulated weather instance uniforms. Backlighting is a neutral transmitted fraction, since native diffuse lighting already multiplies albedo once. There is no emission. Frond snow uses the upward physical surface side, with mirror handling and a lowered crown under accumulation.

The curved frond meshes have 1,936 triangles near and 1,056 in the middle band. Their authored middle meshes keep the frond shape with fewer curved subdivisions. In each local cluster, mature and young ferns switch to their respective middle meshes 14 m beyond its two-meter patch boundary. Geometry is hidden after 80 m. Other plant meshes retain their imported Godot LODs. Plants receive SDFGI as Dynamic geometry without voxelizing fine moving leaves into large opaque cells.

`ridge_understory.tscn` and `terrace_understory.tscn` are separately saved local compositions, with 165 and 132 plants respectively. The ridge uses five small trail-edge banks. The terrace uses four frame-edge banks and leaves its central lake sightline clear. They use the existing terrain field's exact triangle height sampling, leave the authored walking line open, and keep roots outside the terrace paving. Each bank has separate editable MultiMesh groups for the plant types, with wind and surface-weather helpers. The demo instances both scenes at identity under `Lookout/Valley`. `build_clusters.py` rebuilds only these two scenes; it leaves the broad meadow and forest scenes untouched.

For rendered verification, the project's HDR 2D viewport returns a linear half-float image. Convert RGBH/RGBAH readbacks with `Image.linear_to_srgb()` before saving a PNG; saving them directly produces an incorrectly dark display image. Apply that conversion before quantizing to 8-bit. Ordinary sRGB RGB8/RGBA8 captures do not need another conversion.

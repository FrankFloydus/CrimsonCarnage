# Close ground plants

These original modeled plants add a local understory to the demonstration landscape. They use curved, folded leaf and blade geometry rather than opaque leaf cards. No new bitmap art is used. `build_ground_plants.py` saves the physical OBJ meshes; the individual `.tscn` files provide editable material, wind, weather, and visibility settings.

`lady_fern` and `young_fern` have arching rachises, paired pinnae, and individually modeled pinnules that follow each curved frond's plane. The mature plant is approximately 0.93 m high, around waist height on the project's measured 1.73 m test mannequin. `bilberry_shrub` supplies small leaves on upright stems, `wood_sorrel` supplies a low trifoliate herb, and `fescue_bunch` and `sedge_tussock` provide two different folded grass silhouettes. `meadow_panicle` has branched stalks with modeled florets rather than a flat seedhead card. The names describe the intended botanical form rather than a surveyed species reconstruction.

The four shared ShaderMaterials own leaf, dry-leaf and stem colors, roughness, transmission, and vein detail. `ground_plant.gdshader` supports the existing wind and accumulated weather instance uniforms. Backlighting is a neutral transmitted fraction, since native diffuse lighting already multiplies albedo once. There is no emission. Fine vein variation is mathematical shader detail, not an extra image texture.

The fern `_mid` meshes retain a lobed pinna silhouette with fewer separate pinnules. Their rachises and overall canopy footprints match the detailed meshes. In the local cluster scene, mature and young ferns switch to their respective middle meshes 14 m beyond each two-meter patch boundary. Geometry is hidden after 80 m. Other plant meshes retain their imported Godot LODs. Plants receive SDFGI as Dynamic geometry without voxelizing fine moving leaves into large opaque cells.

`ridge_understory.tscn` and `terrace_understory.tscn` are separately saved local compositions, with 238 and 198 plants respectively. They use the existing terrain field's exact triangle height sampling, leave the authored walking line open, and keep roots outside the terrace paving. Each bank has separate editable MultiMesh groups for the plant types, with wind and surface-weather helpers. Instance either scene under `Lookout/Valley` at identity transform to match the authored sites. `build_clusters.py` rebuilds only these two scenes; it leaves the broad meadow and forest scenes untouched.

# Dynamic Sky

Dynamic Sky is a reusable outdoor environment for Godot 4.8 Forward+. The rig owns atmosphere and volumetric clouds, sun and moon lighting, weather profiles and transitions, and camera-relative effects. It uses the sibling engine's Forward+ directional projector for world-anchored sunlight transmission through the cloud layer. [Rig scene](../addons/dynamic_sky/dynamic_sky.tscn) · [Unreal reference map](ultra_dynamic_sky_audit.md)

## Open the demo

Open the project with the [rebuilt editor](../../Godot_RPG.exe), choose **Project → Tools → Open Dynamic Sky Demo**, and press **F6**. Scrub the clock to compare daylight, twilight, and night. Switch between **Open terrace**, **Under shelter**, **Lakeshore**, **Sky survey**, **Weather apron**, and **Ridge trail**. Hold **RMB** to look around, use **WASD** to fly, and use **Q/E** to descend or ascend. [Demo scene](../addons/dynamic_sky/demo/dynamic_sky_demo.tscn)

The active **Lookout → Camera3D** owns an editable **CameraAttributesPractical** resource with bounded automatic exposure: scale **0.17**, minimum sensitivity **34**, maximum sensitivity **800**, and adaptation speed **3**. These values cap the brightening gain at approximately **4×**. The resource is local to each demo instance; the other saved cameras supply view poses. A matched morning, noon, dusk, night, snow, and storm comparison found more highlight detail than the previous exposure. The maximum gain is unchanged, and the 23:30 comparison retained the same average luminance. [Camera exposure resource](../addons/dynamic_sky/demo/lookout_camera_attributes.tres)

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Rain** outside and under the roof | Fine, mostly vertical rain at near, middle, and far distances. The open terrace accumulates water and shows contact splashes. The sheltered foreground suppresses nearby drops and muffles audio. Rain produces distant sheet flashes and thunder automatically. |
| **Snow** and **Blizzard** from **Weather apron** | Irregular, velocity-aligned snow sprites follow cached world-space trajectories, with small lateral twirls and gradual surface coverage. Blizzard increases wind and emission. Optional snow contacts leave short-lived stationary flakes. Physics collision clips trajectories at roofs and terrain. |
| **Storm** from **Open terrace** or **Sky survey** | Stronger wind deflection and surface spray. Visible strikes and sheet flashes occur automatically at variable intervals. Thunder arrives after its sound travel time. **Trigger Lightning** requests a visible strike on demand. |
| **Sandstorm** | Suspended grains, saltating grit at the ground, and larger gust billows within moving mineral haze. Shelter suppresses the near grit. |
| **Lakeshore** during **Rain** | Small ripple impacts affect the lake surface. |

The demo contains saved 3D spruce, ground-cover, and terrain assets. Its 4096 × 4096 material maps are baked from original 1254 × 1254 ImageGen masters. The bake estimates surface relief and roughness from that artwork. Falling snow uses a separate original 887 × 1774 packed atlas, imported as 64 × 128 linear, lossless texture data. [ImageGen sources and PBR bake](dynamic_sky_imagegen.md)

West Ridge uses a 6 km terrain mesh with a sheltered lookout, trail, lake basin, and surrounding mountain ranges. The offline authoring files regenerate the terrain and reanchor the saved forest and grass to its triangles. [Landscape authoring and rebuild steps](../addons/dynamic_sky/demo/landscape/authoring/README.md)

## Cloud and color modes

Use **Sky survey** and **Partly Cloudy** to compare the cloud modes. Keep the camera and time fixed to see which paths respond to wind, then scrub the clock to compare lighting.

| Control | Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud Mode | Volumetric | Ray-marches a world-space formation volume, fine erosion, light transmission, and distance haze. The shared cloud projector filters the Sun on the local Forward+ build. |
| Cloud Mode | 2D Dynamic | Moves a separate layered cloud texture across the dome with perspective distortion and directional shading. Its projector follows the lower sheet. |
| Cloud Mode | Static | Keeps the authored cloud silhouette fixed while the packed lighting channels follow the sun or moon. Its projector uses a stable ground footprint. |
| Cloud Mode | Space | Shows the celestial background and suspends terrestrial fog and precipitation. |
| Color Mode | Sky Atmosphere | Uses the physical scattering and transmittance tables. |
| Color Mode | Simplified Color | Uses separate art-directed sun and moon tables, editable tints, and the authored fog color. |

Select modes under **DynamicSky → Sky Rendering**, or under Configuration when assigned. **Material Default** (`-1`) leaves the actual native Sky Material's current mode untouched; it does not restore a separate cached mode. Explicit root selections apply to newly assigned native Sky/ShaderMaterial resources as well. Runtime callers can use `set_cloud_mode()` and `set_color_mode()`; these keep the root selectors synchronized. Demo startup reads the rig's modes into its menus instead of overriding them. Color selection applies to the terrestrial sky modes. Volumetric quality offers a half-resolution **Balanced** pass and a denser full-resolution **High** pass. West Ridge uses High. [Source mode comparison](ultra_dynamic_sky_cloud_modes.md) · [Sky shader](../addons/dynamic_sky/rendering/dynamic_sky.gdshader)

The Volumetric material uses an original formation field and independent erosion volume with a numerical altitude profile. Coverage changes the cloud base, thickness, and erosion through that profile. Partly Cloudy uses 0.38 coverage; Snow uses the same broken-sky coverage so its flakes remain visible against blue gaps. The directional projector samples the same field without the view's near-camera fade. **Exposure Settings → Cloud Interior Fog Strength** adds nearby scene extinction through native volumetric fog. The sky pass has no distance-aware geometry intersection. [Cloud data and performance](ultra_dynamic_sky_cloud_modes.md#godot-volume-field)

## Add it to a level

Open a scene with a **Node3D** root and choose **Project → Tools → Add Dynamic Sky**. The command inserts the saved rig and supports editor undo. Use a single world environment and let the rig supply the level's main sun and moon lights. The command checks for an existing world environment or directional light before adding the rig. Save the level after adding it. [Editor plugin](../addons/dynamic_sky/plugin.gd)

| To change | Edit |
| --- | --- |
| Clock, latitude, date, or north | **DynamicSky → Clock**. **Day Length Seconds** at zero freezes time. Assign **Astronomy** to use a calendar, longitude, UTC offset, lunar phase, and sidereal stars. The empty setting uses the equinox-style arc and an opposing moon. |
| Starting conditions and automatic changes | **DynamicSky → Weather**. Assign a **Start Weather** resource. Assign **Climate** for weighted seasonal weather changes at real-time, hourly, or daily intervals. |
| Sky colors, cloud shape, aurora, or space | **WorldEnvironment → Environment → Sky → Sky Material → Shader Parameters**. Cloud shadows use the shared lower volume. |
| Light, fog, or temperature | **DynamicSky → Lighting** and **Temperature**. **Lightning Energy** controls the directional scene flash. |
| Lightning distance or impact | **DynamicSky → Lightning Strikes → Lightning Settings** owns strike distance, cloud altitude, timing, and ground collision. |
| Precipitation or airborne mist | **DynamicSky → Precipitation** owns Rain Settings, Snow Settings, camera, collision, and snow contact. **Airborne Weather** owns mist density/color/shelter and Wind Fog Settings. |
| Weather above clouds or beneath water | **Configuration → Exposure Settings**, or **DynamicSky → Weather → Exposure Settings** when Configuration is empty. Local water footprints use saved **WaterRegion** scenes. |
| Audio | **DynamicSky → Weather Audio → Weather Audio Settings** owns shelter, smoothing, sound speed, and thunder range. The root owns the listener override; saved players retain their clips and native attenuation. |

Duplicate a preset for level-specific values. Wind is horizontal velocity in meters per second along world X and Z. **Gustiness** sets its variation. **Lightning Rate** and **Sheet Lightning Rate** set the mean visible and obscured events per real minute, respectively. **Temperature Offset** shifts the air temperature from the clear day/night baseline. [Presets](../addons/dynamic_sky/presets) · [Weather fields](../addons/dynamic_sky/dynamic_sky_weather.gd)

**Daylight Cloud Transport** on a weather profile adds distant atmosphere light and source-calibrated cloud emission to the visible volumetric clouds. The contribution follows the Sun's elevation and blends with the weather profile. It defaults to zero and the UDW comparison's **Snow** preset enables it. It applies in **Volumetric** with **Sky Atmosphere**. The camera, direct scene lights, and lighting cubemap keep their own settings. [Weather profile](../addons/dynamic_sky/dynamic_sky_weather.gd) · [Comparison Snow preset](../addons/dynamic_sky/demo/udw_comparison/weather/snow.tres)

The default **Source Snow and Dust** mode combines depth-bounded snowfall mist with finite dust paths for Calm Dust and Sandstorm. Coarse dust cards and fine spots move through a 20 m camera-biased field. Surface collision clips their paths when enabled. The local dust mist integrates up to **Trace Distance** against scene depth. **Dust Amount** scales the mist, and **Dust Particle Density Scale** controls the emitters. **Calibrate Daylight Dust** coordinates visible sky scattering, distance fog, and sun attenuation in heavy dust with Sky Atmosphere. Select **Source Snow** to use the earlier lit haze and three-layer dust emitters. **Lighting → Moon Light Color** is authored on the root or its Configuration and drives the actual Moon light, including live edits. Its default is sRGB `(0.698462, 0.772014, 0.937938)`, converted from the source's linear `(0.445801, 0.557475, 0.864583)`. Energy, phase, altitude and weather attenuation remain separate. An isolated fixed-exposure night comparison confirmed a less saturated lunar tint; this does not by itself correct the sky and fog's separate night colors. The demo disables its previous contrast/saturation adjustment, retaining the authored numeric values for later grading.

**Fog Amount** is normalized weather data and drives rainbow moisture. Above-cloud exposure filters both Fog Amount and physical **Fog Density**. Enable Rainbow Settings and look away from a low sun in wet weather: the primary red edge is 42° from the anti-solar direction, and the fainter secondary reverses its spectrum around 51°. Clouds, actual sunlight, Space, water, and above-cloud exposure attenuate it. Moisture rises at one unit per second and fades over 100 seconds by default. The final terrestrial full-resolution sky pass adds it once in either color mode. [Rainbow settings](../addons/dynamic_sky/rendering/atmospheric_optics/rainbow_settings.gd) · [Optics shader](../addons/dynamic_sky/rendering/atmospheric_optics/rainbow.gdshaderinc)

**Fog Density** is horizontal extinction in inverse meters. The calibrated presets use `0.000264249` for Clear, `0.000264730` for Partly Cloudy, `0.000330742` for Light Rain, `0.000362772` for Overcast, `0.000399256` for Rain, `0.000660623` for Snow, `0.001217978` for Storm, `0.005164870` for Blizzard, and `0.024286900` for Sandstorm. These coefficients match source horizontal attenuation at the reference fog height. New rigs enable **Drive Height Fog From Weather**, which uses preset falloff and start distance in the custom Forward+ renderer. Local wind mist and the WorldEnvironment's native volumetric fog have their own controls. ### Expose internal nodes in the instance Inspector

Open the reusable scene, select the internal child such as **WorldEnvironment**, and enable **Node → Inspector → Exposed In Scene**. Save the scene. Selecting its instance root in a level shows the child's native Inspector section at the top of the ordinary properties. Dynamic Sky already exposes WorldEnvironment. The checkbox uses the child's nearest owning scene root; it does not expose that child through every ancestor scene. Ownerless roots hide the checkbox. The advanced `Node.inspector_exposed_nodes: Array[NodePath]` list remains available under **Inspector → Advanced** on the owning root. [Node implementation](../../godot/scene/main/node.cpp) · [Native Inspector](../../godot/editor/inspector/editor_inspector.cpp)

Edits target the child directly and use the containing level's undo history. Saving the level stores ordinary instance property overrides. Intermediate ancestors are traversed without saving unrelated changes, and unexposed siblings remain outside this mechanism. Removing an exposure entry hides its controls while retaining existing overrides through a storage-only path list. Empty, missing, absolute, self, ancestor, and property-subname paths are ignored with configuration warnings; repeated invalid paths produce one warning per distinct path.

Expand the **Environment** resource. When it is embedded in the source scene and guarded as a foreign resource, click **Make Editable Copy** above its settings. This explicit action creates a copy for the current scene instance, including internal Sky and ShaderMaterial resources, and records the assignment in scene undo history. The controls become editable; copy assignment and subsequent property edits both support undo and redo. Save the level to retain the overrides. External shaders, textures, and other external resources keep their existing sharing rules, and imported resources remain guarded. A read-only root cannot use this action.

For ordinary external resources, use **Make Unique** when one instance needs independent values, or author the resource with **Local To Scene** enabled when each runtime instance needs a copy. Changing a shared external resource still changes that shared resource. Native child properties remain ordinary per-instance overrides. The rig drives weather-owned fog values and runtime shader uniforms; edit their weather/configuration inputs for persistent weather tuning. Animation keying is disabled in the embedded child Inspector because its existing workflow targets the selected node; use the child's regular Inspector when authoring animation tracks.

The controller adopts a replacement assigned to the actual **WorldEnvironment → Environment**, including a different Sky or ShaderMaterial inside it, on the next normal update. A manual time or weather change also refreshes these references in the editor. Detached resources stop receiving updates; authored rendering flags and unrelated shader parameters on the replacement remain intact. This uses the existing update path without continuous editor polling.

At startup, a saved native child Environment override takes precedence over the configuration's Environment. Without that override, Configuration supplies the initial Environment when assigned. This also preserves a copied Sky or material whose saved values Godot restores into an existing local resource. After startup, assigning a configuration or changing its Environment reference explicitly selects that resource; unrelated configuration edits retain the current native child assignment. Clearing the configuration's Environment restores the scene's initial native Environment. With Configuration empty, the saved native child assignment supplies the startup environment. ### Height fog

On the custom Forward+ build, **WorldEnvironment → Environment → Fog → Height Integrated** evaluates the density along each camera ray through a layer that thins with altitude. **Height** is the reference Y for **Density**, **Height Falloff** is in inverse meters, and **Start Distance** is measured along the ray. The existing Depth mode, sky fog, and volumetric fog have their own controls.

The stock Dynamic Sky scene and default Configuration enable **Drive Height Fog From Weather**. The rig blends each profile's **Fog Height Falloff** and **Fog Start Distance** with the other local weather channels and uses the active Environment's reference height. Turning it off restores the Environment's authored values. Builds without the custom renderer support continue to use their native fog.

### SDFGI probe lighting

On the custom Forward+ build, **WorldEnvironment → Environment → SDFGI → World Space Irradiance** uses world-space directions and solid-angle weights for probe lighting. **Y Scale** still controls the voxel grid's geometry sampling. Changing the setting rebuilds the probe cache and briefly reduces indirect light while the probes converge.

The stock DynamicSky scene, the default Configuration, and the UDW comparison Environment enable it. New standalone Environment resources default to off. A scene with its own Environment uses that resource's setting. [Native Environment properties](../../godot/doc/classes/Environment.xml)

### SDFGI contacts

The custom Forward+ build exposes **SDFGI → Contact Refinement Radius** and **Contact Refinement Strength** on the Environment. The stock rig, default Configuration, and UDW comparison use a 0.9 m radius and full strength. The renderer refines diffuse light in the finest cascade near contacts between ground and other surfaces. The Blizzard comparison uses it to smooth the hard shading band where the cube meets the displaced snow. Set the radius to zero to disable it. SDFGI must be enabled and **Use Occlusion** must be off. An Environment supplied by the scene uses its own values. [Installed editor](../../Godot_RPG.exe) · [Native Environment properties](../../godot/doc/classes/Environment.xml)


### Projected wetness

For a separate wet footprint on an opaque receiver, use a native **Decal** with an albedo texture carrying coverage in alpha, **Albedo Mix** at zero, and the target roughness in the ORM texture's green channel. Select only **Roughness** under **ORM Channels**. Enable **Roughness Affects SSR** to include it in the Forward+ roughness prepass. The demo's weather-responsive materials supply their own rain film and puddles. Use projected wetness on receivers that need a separate authored footprint. ## Local weather and materials

Instance **WeatherZone** and assign a weather resource. Use a box, sphere, or closed `Path3D` with vertical bounds. **Falloff** blends weather outside the region. Higher priorities apply later in overlaps. The active camera samples the local result for the sky and observer effects. Weather-responsive meshes sample the result at their own geometry origin. ### Radial Storm

Place **Radial Storm** in the same World3D as the rig. Its editable **Weather**, **Outer Radius**, **Inner Radius Fraction**, **Storm Alpha**, and **Priority** determine the local blend. The supplied thunderstorm has a 4 km horizontal footprint, with full weight through 2.08 km. A clear profile opens a gap in an overcast Volumetric sky. The cloud renderer, native shadow projector, and in-cloud fog use the same moving field, independently of the camera's local rain. Static and Dynamic 2D use the local scalar coverage blend. [Radial Storm scene](../addons/dynamic_sky/weather/radial_storm.tscn) · [Shared cloud field](../addons/dynamic_sky/rendering/cloud_density.gdshaderinc)

Call `fade_in(seconds)`, `move_over_time(world_target, seconds, fade_out_seconds)`, `cancel_animation()`, or `fade_out(seconds)` for runtime control. ## Rendering and collision

The root **DynamicSky → Precipitation → Snow Settings** resource owns the falling-snow spawn volume, physical sprite scale, trajectory cache, and rates. Stock settings use a camera-biased horizontal disc with 20 m radius and 6 m height, a 1 cm sprite scale randomized from 1 to 2.4 times that size, 2000 cached paths, and a maximum path lifetime of 3 seconds.
